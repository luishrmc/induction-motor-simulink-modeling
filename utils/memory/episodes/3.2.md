## 1. Technical Hurdles & Failure Modes Encountered
- **Coupled Bench Mechanical Drag Accounting:** Applying external nominal shaft load $T_L = 20.602\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ causes total opposing mechanical torque on the rotor to reach $T_{opp} = T_L + B\omega_m = 20.602 + (0.0426 \times 178.07) = 28.188\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$. Under this load, the motor operates at $n = 1700.42\text{ rpm}$ ($s = 0.0553$), delivering useful mechanical shaft power $P_{load} = T_L \omega_m = 3669\text{ W} \approx 3.7\text{ kW}$ while dissipating $1350\text{ W}$ in bench viscous friction.
- **Headless Timeout Extension for Multi-Second Transients:** Simulating an $8.0\text{ s}$ horizon with $50\,\mu\text{s}$ discrete fixed step takes $\sim 129\text{ s}$ of CPU time. The default 120s timeout in `.agents/hooks/matlab-eval.sh` timed out during early tests; resolved by setting `MATLAB_TIMEOUT=${MATLAB_TIMEOUT:-300}`.
- **Instantaneous Power Calculation without Lag:** Computed instantaneous 3-phase active power $p(t) = v_\alpha i_\alpha + v_\beta i_\beta$ and reactive power $q(t) = v_\beta i_\alpha - v_\alpha i_\beta$ via Clarke $\alpha-\beta$ transform, applying a 1-cycle ($16.67\text{ ms}$) running mean for plotting fundamental powers without introducing phase lag.
- **MATLAB LaTeX Typography & String Escapes in Plain Char Vectors:**
  - Double backslashes (`\\`) in plain char vectors (`'...'`) are treated as two literal backslashes by MATLAB and evaluated as linebreaks (`\linebreak`) by the internal LaTeX engine.
  - In Iterations 1 & 2, this caused orphaned tokens in legends and titles (`$\mathrm{s}$`, `$\cdot m$`, and `$\omega_m)$`).
  - Resolved by enforcing single backslashes in plain char vectors (`\mathrm{s}`, `\cdot`, `\omega_m`) and non-breaking math space `~`, while reserving double backslashes (`\\`) exclusively for format specifiers inside `sprintf(...)`.

## 3. Key Architectural Takeaways for Future Sessions
- In MATLAB, plain character vectors `'...'` do not escape backslashes: write `'($\mathrm{N\cdot m}$)'` and `'($B\omega_m$)'`. Never write `'\\'` unless inside `sprintf('...\\cdot...')`.
- For LaTeX non-breaking math spaces, use `~` (e.g. `($t = 5.0~\mathrm{s}$)`) rather than `\ ` or `\\ `.
- Under coupled bench drag $B = 0.0426\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}\cdot\text{s/rad}$, applying nominal shaft load $T_L = 20.602\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ requires $T_e \approx 28.19\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ and line current $I_{line} \approx 18.11\text{ A RMS}$ at $n \approx 1700.4\text{ rpm}$.
