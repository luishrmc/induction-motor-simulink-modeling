## 1. Technical Hurdles & Failure Modes Encountered
- **Premature Horizon Truncation & Tolerance Gate Failure:** Initial test at $t_{stop} = 5.0\text{ s}$ left the high-inertia rotor ($J = 0.701\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$) still slightly accelerating ($n = 1774.8\text{ rpm}$ vs $1775.52\text{ rpm}$), resulting in developed torque $T_e = 8.10\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ vs $7.92\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ (error $3.51\% > 2.0\%$). Resolved by expanding the simulation horizon to $t_{stop} = 6.0\text{ s}$, which allowed full asymptotic settlement to $n = 1775.36\text{ rpm}$ and $T_e = 7.922\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ (error dropped to $0.024\%$).
- **Transcendental No-Load Analytical Operating Point:** At no-load ($T_L = 0$), steady state is governed by the equilibrium between electromagnetic torque and viscous friction drag: $T_{em}(s) = B\omega_m(s) = B\omega_s(1-s)$. Because $T_{em}(s)$ is non-linear, evaluating at nominal slip ($1715\text{ rpm}$) or synchronous speed ($1800\text{ rpm}$) is physically incorrect. Formulated and solved the root equation via `fzero` yielding exact baseline metrics: $s = 0.013603$, $n = 1775.52\text{ rpm}$, $T_{em} = 7.9207\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$, $I_{line} = 8.4928\text{ A RMS}$.
- **Inrush DC Offset vs. Symmetrical Locked-Rotor Baseline:** Direct instantaneous current measurement revealed a peak of $121.25\text{ A}$ at $t = 7.9\text{ ms}$, exceeding the symmetrical theoretical peak ($95.78\text{ A} = \sqrt{2} \times 67.73\text{ A RMS}$) by a factor of $1.266\times$. Reconciled this as the physical asymmetrical DC component from inductive switching ($X/R = 2.31$), which damps within $4\text{--}5$ cycles ($\approx 70\text{ ms}$) into the symmetrical locked-rotor envelope.
- **Figure Production Anti-Occlusion Standards:** Implemented dual-panel figures for current and torque (Subplot 1: full $0\text{--}6\text{ s}$ electromechanical acceleration; Subplot 2: $0\text{--}150\text{ ms}$ / $0\text{--}250\text{ ms}$ subtransient zoom) with explicit LaTeX interpreters, braced accents, and northeast/southeast legend placement preventing any waveform or marker clipping.

## 3. Key Architectural Takeaways for Future Sessions
- Induction motors coupled to motor-generator test benches do not idle at synchronous speed; their true no-load operating point satisfies $T_{em}(s) = B\omega_m(s)$, producing measurable no-load slip ($s \approx 1.36\%$, $n \approx 1775.5\text{ rpm}$, $T_{em} \approx 7.92\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$).
- In heavy inertia systems ($J = 0.701\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$), startup electromechanical settling requires $\ge 4.5\text{ s}$. For Task 3.2 (load torque step), $t_{step} \ge 4.5\text{ s}$ and $t_{stop} \ge 7.0\text{ s}$ are mandatory to establish clean pre-disturbance steady state and subsequent post-step convergence.
- Subtransient inrush waveforms should always be presented alongside zoomed subtransient windows to capture initial DC offset decay and torque pulsation peaks ($78.8\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ at $t = 15\text{ ms}$).
